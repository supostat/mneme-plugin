Staff identity platform

One identity core is the master for staff identity. The HR system writes people into it, workflows turn their attributes into group membership, and every app is a client with its own roles. Developers never hold full admin: the developer portal shows their apps, and structural changes land through Git.

Who owns what

Component Owns Never does
HR system Who exists, their HR attributes, joining and leaving dates Decide app access
Identity core Sign-in, sessions, groups, roles, tokens and lifecycle rules Hold app-specific business data
Git + CI Client definitions, roles, group-to-role mappings and permissions Hold secrets in plain text
Provisioning worker Pushing users out to apps that keep local accounts Decide who gets access

Architecture

Clients, roles and groups

Each app gets one client and defines its own roles on it. Roles are never assigned to people directly: they are mapped onto groups, and people join groups. One group can hold any number of roles across any number of apps.

Rules for every client

  • Client roles, not directory-wide roles, for anything app-specific.
  • Roles are verbs on the app's own nouns, such as shifts.publish, defined by the app team.
  • Site scoping lives in the sites claim, never in role names.

Example token

{
  "azp": "scheduling",
  "employee_type": "salaried",
  "sites": ["S012", "S044"],
  "resource_access": { "scheduling": { "roles": ["shifts.view", "shifts.publish"] } }
}

Joiners, end to end

  1. The HR system creates the user through the inbound provisioning API.
  2. Workflows put the user into the groups the attributes call for.
  3. The provisioning worker creates the account in every app in scope.
Check before relying on it Confirm that an attribute update, not only user creation, triggers a workflow on your exact version, and keep one owner per group branch.

Delegation

Scoped admin permissions grant specific admin actions on specific clients and groups instead of broad administrative roles.

Note Delegate through group membership: user permissions apply to the whole directory, not to a chosen set of users.

Data model

The ERD comes from the real schema: figura erd reads Prisma, TypeORM, schema.rb or the live database and splits a large schema into diagrams that fit the page.

figura erd --source prisma prisma/schema.prisma --out docs/erd --hide-service-columns
select a.email, count(o.id) as orders
from accounts a
left join orders o on o.account_id = a.id
group by a.email;
Decision The HR system is the only writer of HR attributes, so nobody can grant themselves access by editing their own profile.
